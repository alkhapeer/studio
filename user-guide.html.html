Section 1 Introduction
01 · Introduction

What is this tool?

Trend Studio is a web application that runs directly in your browser. It lets you produce Reels, TikToks, and Shorts with professional quality — by assembling short scenes supported by images, text, audio, and visual effects — then exporting the final video with one click.

The core idea

Every idea or trend equals one independent scene with its own background, text, and audio. You chain several scenes together, and they produce a complete final video. No editing experience is required — everything happens through a simple visual interface.

System requirements

Requirement Minimum Recommended
Browser Recent Chrome / Edge / Firefox Chrome 110+ or Edge 110+
Memory 4 GB 8 GB or more
Processor Dual-core Quad-core or better
Display 1280 × 720 1920 × 1080 or higher
Connection None required after loading Stable for the first load
⚠️ Important note

Preview and export both depend on your device's capability. When exporting a long video or a 4K file, keep the tab active and avoid switching to other tabs until export finishes.

What you do NOT need

  • No account and no email address.
  • No editing software installed (Premiere, CapCut, etc.).
  • No file uploads to any external server.
  • No monthly subscription for basic use.

Section 2 Quick Start
02 · Quick Start

8 steps to your first video

This is the fastest way to get started. You will produce a 15-second Reel within five minutes.

  1. Choose an aspect ratio From the top bar, pick 9:16 Reels for Reels, TikTok, and Shorts, or 16:9 for YouTube, or 1:1 for Instagram feed posts.
  2. Choose export quality From the adjacent list, pick 1080p (always recommended), or 4K if your device is powerful and you want the highest quality.
  3. Name your project Type the project name into the "My First Project" field. This name will be used as the final video's filename.
  4. Set up your scenes The tool starts with three ready scenes. Click + Add to add more, or drag any scene in the left list to reorder it. Each scene equals one idea.
💡 When should I add a new scene?

Add a new scene for every distinct idea: a separate trend, a separate question, a separate product, or an independent main point. Never mix two ideas into one scene.

Section 2 Quick Start
  1. Write your scene content From the Scene tab, write: the Hook (a line that grabs attention), the Title, the Description, and the Call to Action (CTA). These four elements appear in a predetermined chronological order inside the scene.
  2. Choose background and effect From the Background tab, pick one of 12 ready-made animated backgrounds (Aurora, Particles, Neon, and more). From the Effects tab, choose a motion (slow zoom, gentle shake, glitch, etc.).
  3. Add an image and audio (optional) From the Image tab, import a supporting image and choose its position. From the Audio tab, import an audio clip — it will loop automatically to fill the scene duration.
  4. Preview and export Press the play button ▶ at the bottom to watch the real preview. When satisfied, click ⬇ Export Video at the top right, and wait for the progress bar to finish.
🎬 What you get

An MP4 file (or WebM depending on your browser) at 1080p quality, ready to upload directly to TikTok, Instagram, or YouTube Shorts.

⏱️ How long does export take?

As a rule of thumb, each second of video requires one to two seconds of export time. A 15-second video takes 15 to 30 seconds. A one-minute video takes about a minute.

Section 3 The Interface
03 · The Interface

A quick tour of the layout

The interface is divided into four clear regions. Learn them once, and you will navigate quickly from then on.

Top bar Scene list (left) Preview (center) Settings panel (right) Timeline (bottom)
1) Top bar

Contains: project name, aspect ratio selector, export quality, and the main export button.

2) Scene list (left)

All scenes in a list. Click any scene to jump to it, drag it to reorder, or delete it with the ✕ button.

3) Preview (center)

The screen that renders your video live. It updates instantly with any change. Below it are the playback controls and timeline.

4) Settings panel (right)

Seven tabs: Scene, Project, Image, Background, Effects, Text, Audio.

5) Timeline (bottom)

Shows the sequence of scenes in relative lengths. Click any part to jump to it instantly.

⌨️ Keyboard shortcuts

Press Space to play or pause the video. Click + Add to add a new scene. Drag any scene in the list to reorder it.

Section 4 The Tabs
04 · The Tabs

The Scene and Project tabs

The Scene tab — the heart of everything

This is where you write the textual content of the current scene. The four fields appear on screen in a predetermined chronological order:

  • Hook: The first line that stops the scroll — appears during roughly the first two seconds.
  • Title: The main heading that clarifies the idea — appears right after the hook.
  • Description: A supporting line that expands on the title — appears in the middle of the scene.
  • Call to Action (CTA): "Follow us" or "Discover more" — appears at the end of the scene.

From this tab you also adjust the scene duration in seconds (from 1 to 60 seconds) and the scene name displayed in the side list.

The Project tab — a professional touch

This tab adds two independent images that appear full-screen:

Type Timing Best used for
Intro image First seconds of the video Channel logo or brand identity
Outro image Last seconds of the video Channel logo, link, or follow invitation

You can set the display duration of each image (0.5 – 8 seconds) and enable or disable it with a toggle switch.

💡 Practical example

Place your channel logo as an intro for 1.5 seconds, then the scenes, then a "Follow now" image as an outro for 2.5 seconds. This gives your video a distinctive visual identity.

Section 5 The Tabs
05 · The Tabs

The Image and Background tabs

The Image tab — a supporting image in the scene

This allows you to show an image inside the scene above the background and below the text. Ideal for:

  • A product photo alongside a title and description.
  • A screenshot or chart supporting the idea.
  • A portrait or logo when mentioning a name.

Image options:

Option Function
Position Top / Below hook / Below description / Bottom / Full screen
Image size Slider from 15% to 100% — each position has a slot the image cannot exceed
Timing mode Whole scene / Start of scene / End of scene / After hook / Custom
Replace text The image appears in place of a chosen text after that text ends
📐 Overlap prevention rule

The tool automatically calculates the maximum height for each position so the image never overlaps with the text. The actual image size adapts to the selected slot.

The Background tab — 12 ready backgrounds + import

Backgrounds come in two categories:

  • Programmatic animated (12): Aurora, Particles, Grid, Waves, Bokeh, Neon, Noise, and four gradient options.
  • Imported: An image or video from your device.

You can also choose a solid color for any scene, or apply the current scene's background to all scenes with a single button.

Section 6 The Tabs
06 · The Tabs

Effects, Text, and Audio

The Effects tab

Two independent effects per scene:

  • Background effect: Zoom in, zoom out, pan left/right/up, shake, glitch, flash, blur.
  • Transition from the previous scene: Fade, slide, wipe, zoom — appears during the first 0.6 seconds of the scene.

The Text tab — fine control

For each of the four texts (hook, title, description, CTA):

  • Enable or hide with a toggle switch.
  • Start and end time in seconds (decimals supported).
  • Entrance animation: fade in, slide up/down, pop, zoom in, typewriter.

The Audio tab

Two independent options:

  • Scene audio: A separate file per scene. Loops automatically if shorter than the scene.
  • Background music for the video: One file extending across all scenes.

For each: volume, fade in, fade out, and mute.

🎵 Audio tip for marketing content

For the most professional mix: background music at 25% + voice-over per scene at 100%. This gives your video a professional rhythm without the music overpowering the voice.

Section 7 Recipes
07 · Recipes

Ready-made recipes for viral reels

Three ready-made structures you can copy directly. Each recipe is designed for a different goal.

Recipe 1 — "Trend of the Day"

  • Scene 1: Hook "This trend went insanely viral!" + Neon background + energetic audio.
  • Scene 2: Title "The real reason" + supporting image + zoom effect.
  • Scene 3: Description "3 reasons nobody talks about" + Zoom transition.
  • Scene 4: CTA "Follow us for the next trend" + outro with your logo.

Recipe 2 — "Quick Tip"

  • Scene 1: Direct question "Are you making this mistake?" + dark background + calm audio.
  • Scene 2: First tip with a supporting image.
  • Scene 3: Second tip with another image.
  • Scene 4: CTA "Save the video to come back to it" + golden background.

Recipe 3 — "Product Ad"

  • Scene 1: The problem + a supporting image of the pain point.
  • Scene 2: The solution (your product) + product image in the center.
  • Scene 3: Brief features + CTA "Order now".
🎯 The golden hook rule

You have only the first two seconds. A successful hook is either a direct question, a surprising number, a warning, or curiosity. Never start with "Hello" or "Today we'll talk about."

Section 8 Troubleshooting
08 · Troubleshooting

Fixing common issues

Preview is not working

  • Click anywhere on the page first (to grant the browser permission for audio playback).
  • Reload the page if you notice the video freezing.
  • Make sure you are not on another tab — rendering pauses when you switch tabs.

The image does not appear

  • Make sure the "Scene image" toggle is enabled and a position is selected.
  • Check the "Timing mode" — the image may appear at a time you have not yet reached.
  • If you chose "Replace text," the image only appears after that text ends.

Audio is not heard

  • Make sure the volume is raised in the "Audio" tab and "Mute" is not enabled.
  • Click on the page before playing (browser policy requirement).
  • During export, audio is not audible in the preview — but it is fully merged into the final video.

Export does not complete

  • Keep the tab active — rendering pauses when the window is minimized.
  • Reduce the duration or choose 1080p instead of 4K.
  • Reload the page if the issue persists, then try again.

Exported file is WebM instead of MP4

This means your browser does not support MP4 recording via MediaRecorder. Solution: use a recent version of Chrome or Edge. You can also convert WebM to MP4 using any free online converter.
