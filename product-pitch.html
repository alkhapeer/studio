The Problem Product Overview
01 · The Problem

Why content creators struggle

Producing a single professional Reel takes the average creator at least two hours: opening editing software, adjusting the aspect ratio, adding text, mixing audio, exporting, then starting over because of a small mistake. It is slow, costly, and requires technical skill.

The real pain

  • Editing software is complex and takes a long time to learn.
  • Ready-made templates are limited and hard to customize.
  • Slow production means less output and fewer chances to go viral.
  • Relying on external designers raises cost and slows the workflow.
  • Existing web tools upload your files to their servers — a privacy concern.
💸 The cost of delay

A serious creator needs to publish 5–10 Reels per week. With traditional tools, that means 15–20 hours of work per week — the equivalent of a part-time job, just for editing.

The Solution Product Overview
02 · The Solution

What is Trend Studio?

Trend Studio is a web application that runs directly in your browser. You write the text, choose a background and effect, add audio, and click export — and you get a professional video at 1080p or 4K quality within seconds.

The core idea in one line

🎯

Every idea equals an independent scene with its own background, text, and audio. Chain the scenes together and you get a final video — as simple as stacking Lego bricks.

What actually happens?

Time Step
Minute 1 Open the tool in your browser — no download, no sign-up.
Minute 2 Write the text of your first scene (hook + title + description + CTA).
Minute 3 Choose the animated background and visual effect.
Minute 4 Add an image and audio, adjust timings.
Minute 5 Click export, then upload to TikTok or Instagram.
✅ The result

A professional-quality Reel with no editing software, and without uploading your files to any external server. Everything happens on your own device.

Customer FAQ Product Overview
08 · Frequently Asked Questions

Questions buyers ask

Does it work on mobile?

Yes, it works in mobile browsers, but performance is best on a desktop. For the fastest result, use Chrome on a laptop.

Do I need an internet connection?

Only for the first load. After that, you can use the tool offline as long as you don't close the tab.

Is my data stored by you?

We never see your data. Everything happens on your device. No uploads, no account, no tracking.

Can I sell the videos I make?

Yes, without restrictions. The content you produce is entirely yours — you may use it commercially.

Can I add my channel logo?

Yes, through the "Project" tab — you can place one image in the intro and another in the outro, with adjustable durations.

What is the final video format?

MP4 in modern browsers, or WebM as a fallback. Both are accepted on all social media platforms.

Is there an enterprise plan?

Yes, with volume discounts and the option to customize the interface with your company logo and integrate with internal systems.

Can I try the tool before buying?

Yes, the free tier is always available — it produces 1080p video with a small watermark, so you can confirm the output quality before upgrading.
