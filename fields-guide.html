Section 1 Introduction
01 · Introduction

Classification method

Not all fields are equal. Some are where the tool shines with no competitor, some are possible with caveats you must know, and others need an entirely different set of tools. This classification is based on four criteria:

The four criteria

1
Structural fit
2
Duration & memory
3
Interaction type
4
Output quality

1) Structural fit

The tool is built around "sequential scenes." Any field that breaks down into 3–15 short ideas is ideal. Fields that need non-linear flow or interaction are unsuitable.

2) Duration and memory

The tool renders each frame in the browser before encoding. Ideal duration: 15 seconds to 3 minutes. Possible duration: 3 to 10 minutes. Longer than that: use professional editing software.

3) Interaction type

The tool produces linear video (watch-only). Any field requiring clicks, answers, or scoring is unsuitable.

4) Output quality

The tool outputs 1080p or 4K with clear typography. Fields requiring complex graphics or multiple layers are possible with caveats.

🎯 The golden rule

If your field breaks into "one idea + 4 text elements + an image + audio" per scene, the tool is ideal. Anything beyond that belongs in a different tool.

Section 2 Ideal Fields
02 · Ideal Fields

Where the tool has no competitor

🎬

Reels / TikTok / Shorts

15–90 sec 2–6 scenes

This is the field the tool was designed for. Each idea becomes a short scene with a strong hook, text, image, and audio. The ideal duration is 15–45 seconds for the highest completion rate.

Typical structure: Scene 1 (hook) → Scenes 2–4 (content) → Scene 5 (CTA).

Why ideal: 9:16 aspect ratio ready, animated backgrounds fit, per-scene audio, fast export.

🛍️

Short product ads

20–40 sec 3–4 scenes

Classic structure: problem → solution → features → CTA. With the product image in the middle, and background music at 25% + voice-over at 100%.

Why ideal: The "replace text with image" option shows the product automatically after its title, making the ad professional without editing.

📰

News and trend summaries

40–90 sec 5–8 scenes

Each news item = an independent scene. Title first, then details, then a "Follow us for the next trend" CTA. Ideal for news pages and trend accounts.

Why ideal: Easy to add 10 scenes, drag to reorder them, and apply a unified background to all with one button.

Section 2 Ideal Fields
💡

Quick tips (micro-learning)

30–90 sec 3–5 scenes

"5 tips in 60 seconds" — one tip per scene. Ideal for micro-education, quick training, and educational content. Short sentence + number + solution = a perfect recipe.

Why ideal: Each tip has its own background and color, and fast transitions keep attention. Large text reads easily on mobile.

🎉

Occasion greetings

10–25 sec 2–3 scenes

Ramadan, Eid, national days, congratulation messages. Use the intro image as a logo, then a greeting scene, then the outro.

Why ideal: Independent intro and outro images make the video look professionally produced in under 5 minutes.

✅ Ideal fields summary Reels — Product ads — News summaries — Quick tips — Occasion greetings.
All break into "one idea per scene" and need 15–90 seconds. The tool is the fastest alternative on the market for them.

Section 3 Possible with Caveats
03 · Possible with Caveats

Where you need to know the limits

🧠

Quizzes (linear video)

10 sec per question 1 scene per question

Possible as linear video, not as an interactive assessment tool. Each question = one scene:

  • Question scene: Hook = "Question 1" + Title = the question + Duration = thinking time (e.g., 10 sec).
  • Answer scene: Hook = "Answer" + Title = the correct answer + green background.

Limitations: No click input, no auto-scoring, no grade calculation. Suitable for social media challenges, not for formal assessments.

Alternative for formal assessment: Google Forms, Typeform, Quizizz, Moodle.

📈

Excellence pulse reports (KPI display)

60–120 sec 5–10 scenes

Possible to present final results, not as an interactive measurement tool. Correct uses:

  • Recognize an outstanding employee: photo + name + achievement + celebratory music.
  • Opening video for an annual awards ceremony.
  • Monthly performance report in cinematic form (5 KPIs = 5 scenes).

Limitations: Does not collect data, calculate KPIs, or compare based on user input.

Alternative for actual measurement: Power BI, Google Data Studio, Excel.

Ideal workflow: Use a survey tool to collect data, then use Trend Studio to present the result as a professional video.

Section 3 Possible with Caveats
⏱️

Videos of 3–10 minutes

3–10 min 10–40 scenes

Technically possible, but with three limitations you must know:

Limitation Detail Practical workaround
Memory 10 min × 30 fps = 18,000 frames drawn in memory before encoding Keep 3–5 minutes as a safe ceiling
Tab stability The tab must stay active during the entire export Do not switch tabs; keep the charger connected
4K quality 4K × 10 min on a weak device = dropped frames 1080p for long videos, 4K for short ones

Best suited for: Educational lessons, meeting summaries, training modules, filmed podcasts.

Practical recommendation: Split the long video into chapters (3–5 scenes each), export each chapter separately, then merge them with any simple editing tool.

⚠️ Possible fields summary Linear quizzes — Excellence reports — Long videos (3–10 min).
All possible, but be aware of the limitations: no interaction, no measurement, no long duration with 4K.

Section 4 Unsuitable Fields
04 · Unsuitable Fields

What this tool is not for

Being honest with customers is better than making promises we cannot keep. These fields require completely different tools, and we point you to the right alternative.

Field Why unsuitable Suggested alternative
Interactive graded quiz No answer input, no auto-scoring, no grading Google Forms / Typeform / Quizizz
Dynamic dashboard Cannot display data that changes per user Power BI / Looker Studio
Complex editing No keyframes, multi-layer, or masking support Premiere / DaVinci / CapCut
Videos longer than 10 minutes Memory and browser pressure prevent completion Split into chapters + external merge
3D / advanced motion graphics No 3D meshes, particles, or physics After Effects / Blender
Live streaming The tool produces a recorded file, not a stream OBS Studio / Streamlabs
Meeting recording Does not record camera or screen Zoom / Loom / OBS
Importing and editing existing video Does not trim existing video — builds from scenes CapCut / Premiere
💬 A message to customers

Trend Studio is not a replacement for every production tool — it is a replacement for the slow daily production of Reels. When you need something else, ask us and we will point you to the right tool.
