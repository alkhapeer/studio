Manual installation

How to install the app

If the install button doesn't appear, follow the steps below for your device. Instructions are available in English and Arabic.

  1. Open this website in Google Chrome on your Android device.
  2. Tap the three‑dot menu ⋮ in the top‑right corner of the browser.
  3. Select “Install app” or “Add to Home screen”.
  4. Confirm by tapping “Install”.
  5. The Trend Studio icon will appear on your home screen — open it like any app.
  1. Open this website in Safari on your iPhone or iPad.
  2. Tap the Share button ⤴ at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Scroll down and select “Add to Home Screen”.
  4. Give the app a name (or keep Trend Studio) and tap “Add”.
  5. The app icon will appear on your home screen — open it like any app.
⚠️
ImportantInstallation on iPhone and iPad is only possible through Safari. Other browsers do not support adding to the home screen.
  1. Open this website in Chrome, Edge or Brave on your computer.
  2. Look for the install icon ⊕ in the address bar (right side).
  3. Click it, then confirm by clicking “Install”.
  4. The app will open in its own window and appear in your applications list.
